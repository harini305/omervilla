Omer Villa and Retreat

 

Our villa is a modern yet soulful sanctuary with a deep respect for Balinese traditions. Omer Villa boasts spacious living areas, carefully curated interiors, and stunning outdoor spaces where you can unwind and reconnect with nature. Whether you’re lounging by the pool, enjoying a quiet moment in the garden, or watching the breathtaking sunrise, every corner of the villa is designed to offer relaxation and peace.

5 Bedrooms

Each room is thoughtfully designed to create a calming atmosphere, with king-sized beds, en-suite bathrooms, and private terraces that overlook the natural beauty of Bali.

Private Pool

Dive into our sparkling private pool, facing rice field view and tropical greenery, offering the perfect space for both relaxation and fun.

Open-Air Living Spaces

Enjoy Bali’s warm breezes in our open-plan living and dining areas, perfect for family gatherings or intimate dinners with friends.
