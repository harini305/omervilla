Celebrate Life’s Moments – Perfect Events Begin at Omer Villa & Retreat

 

Omer Villa & Retreat offers the perfect setting for your wedding or special event, surrounded by the serene beauty of Bali’s lush rice fields. With its cozy, casual, and intimate ambiance, the villa creates a warm and inviting atmosphere for gatherings. Imagine exchanging vows against the backdrop of stunning green rice field or hosting an unforgettable celebration in a relaxed yet elegant space.

We provide an all-inclusive package to make your event seamless and stress-free, covering everything from transportation and planning to food catering, entertainment, and more. Plus, we offer tailor-made options based on your budget and specific requirements, ensuring every detail is exactly as you envision it. Whether it’s a small, private ceremony or a laid-back event with friends and family, Omer provides a unique venue where you can celebrate life’s special moments in the heart of nature.
