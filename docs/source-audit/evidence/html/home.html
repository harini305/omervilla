Location

Omer Villa & Retreat is nestled in the peaceful Buduk Village, just north of Canggu, Bali, offering an idyllic escape for those seeking tranquility, eco-friendly living, and authentic Balinese experiences. The villa is surrounded by lush rice fields, providing guests with a serene and private retreat while still being conveniently close to popular attractions.

Just a short drive away, you’ll find Pererenan Beach, Batu Bolong Beach, Echo Beach, Cemagi Beach, Seseh Beach, and Kedungu Beach, renowned for their world-class surf breaks and vibrant beach bars. The trendy cafés, boutiques, gym, and yoga studios of Canggu are also easily accessible, offering a perfect blend of relaxation and modern amenities.

For those seeking cultural immersion, the iconic Tanah Lot Temple is just a 20-minute drive from the villa, where you can witness breathtaking sunsets over the Indian Ocean. Ubud, Bali’s cultural heart, is about an hour’s drive away, offering opportunities to explore traditional arts, dance performances, and stunning landscapes.

Omer Villa & Retreat provides the ideal base to experience the natural beauty and cultural richness of Bali, whether you’re looking for relaxation, adventure, or a bit of both.
