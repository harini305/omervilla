[h2] Food & Beverage
[h6] Fresh, Local, and Flavorful — Taste the Essence of Bali at Omer Villa & Retreat.
  At Omer Villa & Retreat, we believe that great food begins with the finest ingredients. Our menu is crafted with love, featuring healthy, homemade dishes made from locally sourced produce. Whether you’re enjoying a vibrant breakfast, a light lunch, or a hearty dinner, you’ll savor the freshness and flavor of every bite. From tropical fruits to organic vegetables and free-range meats, we prioritize sustainability and support local farmers to bring you meals that are as nourishing as they are delicious.
  In addition to our private chef service, we also offer in-villa dining and accept food catering for special events, ensuring that every meal is tailored to your group’s preferences. Enjoy the best of Western and Indonesian cuisine without ever leaving the comfort of your villa.
