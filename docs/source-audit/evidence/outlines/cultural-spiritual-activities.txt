[h2] Cultural & Spiritual Activities
  Our villa is located in the traditional Balinese village of Buduk, offering you the opportunity to immerse yourself in the local culture. Guests are encouraged to participate in authentic Balinese rituals and ceremonies, providing a deeper connection to the island’s rich heritage. From the Melukat purification ceremony and making traditional offerings to local cooking classes, your experience at Omer Villa will be both enriching and unique.
