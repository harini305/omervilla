[h2] Family & Groups
[h6] Create Unforgettable Memories – Your Family’s Escape Awaits at Omer Villa & Retreat
  Omer Villa & Retreat is a family-friendly and group accommodation nestled between Canggu and Ubud, offering a peaceful escape in the heart of Bali. Surrounded by lush rice fields, the villa provides a spacious and serene setting ideal for families, friends, or groups seeking a private retreat.
  Designed with comfort and eco-friendly living in mind, Omer Villa offers ample space for both relaxation and recreation. Kids can enjoy exploring the garden or playing in the outdoor areas, while adults unwind by the pool or take in the tranquil views. With its close proximity to the beaches of Canggu and the cultural richness of Ubud, Omer Villa is the perfect base for exploring Bali’s vibrant landscapes, whether you’re surfing, sightseeing, or simply relaxing as a family.
  With multiple bedrooms, large common areas, and plenty of outdoor space, Omer Villa & Retreat is well-suited for gatherings, reunions, or group vacations, making it a versatile choice for those seeking a blend of comfort, nature, and adventure.
